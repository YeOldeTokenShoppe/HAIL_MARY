EL OJO — THREE PARALLAX LAYERS, VERSION 1

All layer PNGs use a matching 1254 x 1254 square canvas. Stack with matching dimensions and origins:
1. 01-background.png — opaque painted ceremonial chamber, four masked witnesses, unfinished pyramid and watching eye.
2. 02-el-ojo.png — transparent main crimson-robed figure, both gloves, offered coin AND the close marble tabletop.
3. 03-foreground.png — transparent sparse engraved paper fragments.

The close tabletop and its reflections intentionally stay with the figure, so the contacting fingers and coin remain connected to the surface during parallax.
Keep transparent margins. Put existing card text, badges and frame above the art as fixed UI.
Suggested starting values at 400 px artwork width: common scale 1.06; background +/-2 px, subject/table +/-5 px, paper fragments +/-9 px. Clip to the artwork window and tune for your camera.

concept-master.png is the full illustration used to generate the layers.
preview.html displays the actual three-layer stack at rest.
combined-preview.png is a browser-rendered preview; use the three full-resolution layer PNGs for the game.
Generated separation reconstructs hidden areas and can differ slightly from the concept master.

Tool: built-in image_gen. Complete master and layer prompts: prompts.txt.
