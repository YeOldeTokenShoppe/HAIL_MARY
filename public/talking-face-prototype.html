Tune the rig

What you're looking at

  • The mouth opens along the line in your artwork. The lips do most of the moving; the chin and beard follow only a little.
  • Mouth shape changes with the sound: wider on bright sounds like "ee", rounder on dark ones like "oo".
  • The eye blinks, and the head sways and nods with the voice.
  • The mic is masked out so it stays put. With your separated face, beard and prop layers, that mask goes away.
