Trading Desk rules

Be the first to bank 1,000 Prosperity. Liquidity is the money you spend to play cards. Banked Prosperity is your score.

Choose Devotion or Degen, take a seat, then select Deal opening hand. Each player starts with five cards, 5 Liquidity, and an empty Trader seat.

Your turn

  1. Collect income. Your seated Trader earns their LIQ, plus 1 at full Grace. An empty or disabled seat earns 1. The table handles this automatically.
  2. Draw a card. Tap your deck. An Event marked “fires on draw” resolves immediately, then you draw a replacement.
  3. Choose one main action. Play a card and pay its cost, sell a holding, pay 3 Liquidity to clear one of your Calamities, attempt a Hail Mary, or pass. Some cards give you another choice, such as uncorking La Cava. Reading a card is free.
  4. Finish your turn. If you have more than seven cards in hand, choose cards to discard until you have seven.
Traders, the bench, and Grace

Your first Trader takes the seat. Up to two more can wait on the bench. Once per turn, you may swap in a benched Trader for free before your main action. A Trader who loses all their Grace is disgraced unless a card saves them. Lighting a candle restores up to 3 Grace to your seated Trader.

Assets and your portfolio

An Asset you buy goes into your portfolio. Starting on your next turn, you can use your main action to sell it for Liquidity to spend or Prosperity to bank. Inspect the holding to see its current payouts. Some sales depend on a die roll. Rug Pull blocks sales until cleared.

Events, Calamities, and Artifacts

Some Events fire when drawn. Others stay in your hand until you play them. A persistent Calamity sits on the affected desk until cleared. Use a matching Remedy or pay to clear one from your own desk.

Relics give ongoing effects. You normally have two Relic slots and one separate Location. Graces let you make offerings at the shrine. Each card describes its effect.

Candles and the Hail Mary

Once per game, you may spend your main action to attempt a Hail Mary. With zero, one, two, or three lit candles, the usual odds are 1, 2, 4, or 8 chances in 12. Your desk shows the odds after card effects. An answered prayer clears your Calamities and banks 200 Prosperity. A miss spends the attempt.
