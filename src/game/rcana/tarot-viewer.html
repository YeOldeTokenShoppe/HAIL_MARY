HOW TO PLAY IN SIX LINES
  1. Goal: be the first to put 80 Profit in your Bank. Banked Profit can never be touched. Profit in your Portfolio is exposed until you bank it at the start of your turn.
  2. Liquidity pays for cards. Once a turn, Reserve a card you don't need: it becomes a permanent Liquidity point that refreshes every turn. Early on, reserve every turn.
  3. Personalities earn. Hire one (it rests the turn it arrives), then put it to Work each turn for its Yield. A Working Personality is the only thing the bot's Chains can hit.
  4. The Four C's: Coins give Profit now. Candles make a Personality Work for a bonus. Cups give the bot a little and you more, and draw a card. Chains attack a Working Personality, Front-run the bot's Portfolio, or intercept a pass; or stay in hand to Hedge when you're attacked. Reversed Chains are Collateral: armor and extra Yield for one of your own Personalities.
  5. The Majors are fate. One is revealed each round for everyone. Markets stay, Events hit at once, Invokes sit in Providence for whoever pays.
  6. Hail Mary Pass: once per game, throw your whole Portfolio into the air instead of banking. If nothing touches it by the start of your next turn, it banks doubled. If anything does, it's gone.
