HOW TO PLAY IN SIX LINES
  1. Goal: be the first to put 80 Profit in your Bank. Banked Profit can never be touched. Profit in your Portfolio is exposed until you bank it at the start of your turn.
  2. Liquidity pays for cards. Once a turn, drag a card you don't need onto your Reserve: it becomes a permanent Liquidity point that refreshes every turn. Early on, do this every turn.
  3. Personalities earn. Drag a court onto your Floor to hire it (it rests the turn it arrives), then tap it each turn to Work for its Yield. A Working Personality is the only thing the bot's Chains can hit.
  4. The Four C's: Coins give Profit now (drop on your Floor). Candles make a Personality Work for a bonus (drop on it). Cups give the bot a little and you more, and draw a card (drop on the bot). Chains attack a Working Personality, Front-run the bot's Portfolio, or intercept a pass (drop on the target); or stay in hand to Hedge when you're attacked. Reversed Chains are Collateral: drop on your own Personality for armor and extra Yield.
  5. The Majors are fate. One is revealed each round for everyone. Markets stay, Events hit at once, Invokes sit in Providence; tap one to pay for it.
  6. Hail Mary Pass: once per game, throw your whole Portfolio into the air instead of banking. If nothing touches it by your next Set, it banks doubled. If anything does, it's gone.

The coach line under each decision says what the strong bot would do and why. Press "Do that" to follow it, or ignore it and see what happens.
