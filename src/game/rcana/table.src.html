HOW TO PLAY IN SIX LINES
  1. Three numbers. Liquidity pays for cards. Portfolio is Profit you have earned but can still lose. Bank is Profit nobody can touch. First to 80 in the Bank wins.
  2. Your turn has three steps. START: Profit arrives (Dividend, plus a bonus for a big Portfolio), then you bank some or all of your Portfolio. DRAW one card. MAIN: play cards, then End turn.
  3. Getting Liquidity. Once a turn, drag a card you don't need onto Reserve. It's gone, but it pays 1 Liquidity every turn from now on. Spent Liquidity comes back next turn. Do this every turn early on.
  4. Personalities earn. Drag a face card onto your Floor to hire it (it rests the turn it arrives). Each turn after, tap it to Work: its earnings go to your Portfolio. A Working Personality is the only thing the bot's Chains can hit.
  5. Your Network earns. Drag a face card onto your Network to partner with it, at most two at a time (it rests the turn it arrives). Each turn after, tap it to Trade: its earnings go to your Portfolio. Once a turn you may also Copy trade: tap one of the bot's Trading Personalities, pay 1 Liquidity, and take its full earnings; the bot gets 1 for being followed. A Trading Personality is the only thing Chains can hit.
  6. The four suits, as printed on each card. Coins EARN Profit now. Candles BOOST one of your Personalities for extra earnings. Chains ATTACK a Personality that is earning, or the bot's Portfolio, or stay in hand to HEDGE when you are attacked. Cups SHARE: the bot gets a little, you get more and draw a card.
  7. Favor. Every time you Share (a Cups card) or Copy trade, Our Lady of Perpetual Profit notices: +1 Favor. Spend 1 Favor to Foretell: look at the next card of the deck and bury it if you don't like it. Hold 2 Favor and Our Lady spares you from the next Event that would take your Profit or your pass, taking the Favor instead. When THE WORLD turns up, the most favored Trader banks everything.
  8. The last play (the "last-play Hail Mary" box, on by default). You may only throw a Hail Mary when the bot could win by banking at its next turn. Everything exposed goes up, no cap. Nobody wins while it is in the air: the bot banks on its turn and hunts the pass with Chains; at the start of your next turn the pass lands, doubles if untouched, and the larger Bank wins. THE HAIL MARY (The Fool) is the only way to throw early.
  9. Trader cards (the "Trader cards" box, on by default: an 88-card deck). Eight one-shot moves of your own are shuffled in with the Minors. A MOVE is played from your hand like a pip, for its cost. A REACTION is free and fires by itself the first time its moment comes: Stop-Loss caps a big loss at 2, Bailout saves a Personality from Liquidation, Short Squeeze turns a Front-run back on the bot, Circuit Breaker buries an Event that would hurt you. Tap a reaction card to hold it back for a better moment. The bot cannot see what you hold.
  10. Version 1c. The Majors are their own deck: the face-down Omen turns over at the start of every round, before anyone banks, so an Event hits both players in the same spot. Foretell looks at the Omen. Hedging is free: the Chain is spent, no Liquidity. A Hail Mary stake is capped at 20; the rest stays exposed. At two partners you may drag a new face card onto one of them to replace it (its Exit Scam applies). Favor comes from Sharing only.
  11. Fate and the Hail Mary. A Major card turns up for both players every round: Markets stay, Events hit at once, Invokes wait in Providence for whoever pays. Once per game, with 10 or more in your Portfolio, you may throw it all into the air at the start of your turn instead of banking: untouched until the start of your next turn, it banks doubled; touched, it is gone. The box under your numbers tells you when it's ready.

The coach line under each decision says what the strong bot would do and why. Press "Do that" to follow it, or ignore it and see what happens. In the Claude app you can also type a question into "Ask the coach" below your Reserve; it answers from the rules and the table in front of you.
